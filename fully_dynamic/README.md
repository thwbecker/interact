# fdkern: 2-D space-time BIEM prototype for fully dynamic earthquake sequences

Python prototype for the fully dynamic extension of `rsf_solve` (plan and
status in fd_plan.md). Plane strain (in-plane, mode II) and antiplane,
homogeneous full space, straight segments, rate-and-state or
slip-weakening friction, quasi-dynamic (QD) and fully dynamic (FD) phases on
the same operators. Everything is numpy/scipy; no compiled code.

## Layout

Kernels
- `inplane.py`: in-plane segment kernels. Production engine: closed-form
  primitives of Tada and Madariaga (2001, eqs A4-A6; Ando et al. 2007 eqs
  4-6). Cross-check engine: quadrature of a point field derived from the
  3-D Green's function (`gen_inplane.py` generates `inplane_kernel.py` and
  verifies the derivation). `W_table` builds the dynamic-correction rows
  W[l] = K[l] - Ks dt, switching to the large-time series of
  `inplane_series.py` (generated by `gen_series.py`) once beta t / r > 10.
- `antiplane.py`: the antiplane analogue (Ando et al. 2007 eqs 30-31 as
  production engine, derived point field as cross-check).
- `ref_ando2007.py`: the transcribed primitives.

System and time marching
- `shells.py`: `Fault` (straight segment, equal elements), `PairTable`
  (windowed W tables stored as FFTs along the Toeplitz offset axis; parallel
  segment pairs only), `History` (ring buffers of FFT'd slip-rate slices on
  the fine grid), `System` (static operators Ks/Kn, pair tables, history
  convolution, first-lag operator K[1] and cumulative kernels K_k).
- `stepper.py`: FD stepper with end-of-step collocation and variable step
  k dt_min; friction models `PrescribedTip` (self-similar benchmark),
  `SlipWeakening` (linear, optional forced nucleation, exact banded
  active-set solve of the coupled first-lag system), `RateState` (aging
  law, regularized, banded Newton in log V, predictor-corrector in state).
  QD mode replaces K[1] by radiation damping.
- `cycle.py`: sequences. Adaptive QD integrator (scipy DOP853 on slip and
  state, elementwise bracketed Newton closure), backslip loading in shear
  and normal stress, QD to FD hand-off at a slip-rate threshold with the
  fine-grid history rebuilt from the QD dense output, variable FD steps,
  FD to QD return after one window below a lower threshold. `dynamic=False`
  runs the same sequence quasi-dynamically.

Drivers and references
- `run_tpv205.py`, `ref/`: SCEC TPV205-2D and E. Dunham's MDSBI 6.25 m
  on-fault series (stations -4.5, -7.5, +12 km).
- `run_hd.py`: Harris and Day (1993) parallel stepover experiments.
- `run_cycle1.py`: single fault with a 16 km velocity-weakening patch.
- `compare_qd_fd.py`: runs QD and FD sequences on that fault and draws the
  comparison figure (resumable; `--plot-only`, `--only fd|qd`).
- `spectral_ap.py`: independent spectral BIEM (Perrin-Rice-Zheng kernel)
  for the antiplane self-similar crack; shares no code with the above.

Tests (`pytest -q`): `test_inplane.py`, `test_antiplane.py`,
`test_shells.py`, `test_stepper.py` run in about 20 s plus the TPV205-2D
case (20 s); `test_cycle.py` about 2 min; `test_hd.py` about 2 min.

## Conventions

- Segment: centre X, unit tangent s, normal n = rot90(s). Slip positive
  when the +n side moves along +s (right-lateral for s = +x, n = +y).
- Stresses are stress-from-slip; interact's Is/In (stress-drop convention)
  differ by a global sign.
- Effective normal stress sigma = sigma0 - (Kn delta + history), compression
  positive.
- K[l], l >= 1: traction at l dt from unit slip rate during [0, dt).
  tau(t_{n+1}) = sum_{m<=n} K[n+1-m] V^m, so the current step enters through
  K[1] (or K_k for a step of k dt_min), whose diagonal is about -1.46 eta at
  beta dt = dx/2 and whose row sum is exactly -eta.
- Window per pair: last lag at which |W| exceeds tol times the static
  interaction envelope mu dx^2 dt / (2 pi (1-nu) r^2) (an envelope rather
  than the static value because the radiation pattern has nodal lines).

## Validation summary

- Kernels: two independent routes agree to 1e-13 (in-plane) and 1e-14
  (antiplane) away from the fronts; static limits, plane-wave identity,
  early-time self value -eta, P radiation only from segment ends.
- Time marching, exact: symmetrically expanding crack. Mode III factor
  1/E(k), k^2 = 1 - (v/beta)^2 (Broberg 1999 eq. 6.9.145); mode II factor
  2(1-k^2)(k^2-b^2)/g2(b) (eqs 6.9.85-87). Reproduced to 1e-3 at dx = 100 m;
  the spectral solver agrees independently.
- Time marching, community: TPV205-2D at 100/50/25 m against the 6.25 m
  reference: main-front arrivals within 1 to 3 time steps, final slip within
  0.1 to 0.4 percent at 25 m, rms slip difference 0.030/0.019/0.011 m, peak
  slip rates within 3 to 14 percent at 25 m.
- Fault interaction: Harris and Day (1993) case D reproduced (0.5 km steps
  jumped in both senses with their delays and locations; 1 and 2 km not);
  case A delays and locations within 0.2 s and 0.4 km for dilational 1, 2,
  4 km and compressional 1, 2 km. Differences: our case A front runs near
  the P-wave speed (their average 4.2 km/s) and a compressional 4 km step is
  triggered in ours; both robust to dx, nucleation radius and speed;
  attributed to their grid (coarser than L_c) and damping, unconfirmed.
- Sequences: switch threshold insensitivity (1 percent); FD versus QD on
  the test fault: recurrence 82.9 against 68.1 yr, mean slip 1.15 against
  0.89 m, peak slip rate 2.65 against 1.05 m/s.

## Known limitations

- Parallel segments only (Toeplitz tables); non-parallel pairs need dense
  tables (not built).
- Full space only; no free surface.
- The 2-D window is long (1.3 to 5 S-travel times across a pair); window
  sensitivity of jump outcomes untested.
- Near-front values of the derived point field lose accuracy within 1e-8
  relative time of an arrival (cross-check engine only).
- Performance: history convolution O(L M) per pair and step in numpy; see
  fd_plan.md section 3 for timings.
